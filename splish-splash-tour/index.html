10:00

Gates open: burn the headliners

These three build the longest queues by noon. Bootlegger's Run and Riptide Racer only need 42″, but Bombs Away is a hard 48″ — sort that out before anyone climbs fifty feet of stairs.

  1. 01

    Bootlegger's Run

    Intense4-person raftH2Go

    A roller coaster without rails: hydromagnetic tech shoves a four-person raft uphill between the drops and sweeping turns. Best ride in the park, worst line by lunchtime — this is stop one, no debate.

    • Height42″
    • 42–48″Adult on the raft
    • Weight700 lb per raft
  2. 02

    Bombs Away

    IntenseDrop capsule

    Two AquaLaunch capsules, fifty feet up, right above Riptide Racer. You get a countdown, the floor disappears, and you free-fall at 26 feet per second down 300 feet of slide. The whole thing lasts about eleven seconds.

    • Height48″
    • Weight300 lb
    • Duration~11 sec
  3. 03

    Riptide Racer

    ModerateSide-by-side racerSpeedy Pass

    A carnival game blown up to waterslide scale — race the person next to you, settle it in one run. It's directly below Bombs Away, so do them back to back while we're already at that tower.

    • Height42″
    • Weight300 lb
    • FormatRace a friend

11:30

The dark block

Three enclosed rides in a row, all 48″ and up. If anyone hates pitch-black tubes, this is the stretch to sit out — send them to Soak City and regroup at 12:45.

  1. 04

    Dr. Von Dark's Tunnel of Terror

    Intense2-person raftH2Go

    One of only three slides like it in the country. You drop through a waterfall to start, then spin a full 360 in total darkness before a 40-foot drop you never see coming. Take a partner — it's a two-person raft.

    • Height48″
    • Weight400 lb combined
    • Drop40 ft
  2. 05

    Alien Invasion

    IntenseFamily raft funnelH2Go

    The one ride with no raft to carry — a conveyor belt hauls it up for you. Quick drop through the dark, then you spin freely in the funnel with no idea which pass drops you out the bottom. Nothing else in the park feels like it.

    • Height48″
    • Weight700 lb combined
    • PerkNo raft to carry
  3. 06

    Dragon's Den

    IntenseEnclosed tubeH2Go

    Catapults you through twisting darkness into a mist-shrouded bowl, circling past the dragon before it spits you out. The third dark ride in a row, and the one people underrate.

    • Height48″
    • Weight400 lb combined
    • LightsNone

12:45

Food, then float

Eat before the one o'clock crush, then take one full lap of the river to digest. This is the regroup point — anyone who wandered off finds us here.

Waves

Two wave pools, not interchangeable. Kahuna Bay has the four-foot sets; Surf City is the classic and the better place to actually hear each other. Anyone under 48″ has to wear a lifejacket in both.

If anyone brings kids

The shallow end of the park

Five kid-scaled areas, all capped at 54″ and all requiring an adult along. Any of them works as a base camp if the group needs to split for an hour.

  • Stingray Bay

    The all-new kids zone: kid-sized slides, interactive play features, silly sprayers around every corner and a dump bucket overhead.

  • Pirates Cove

    An interactive pirate ship with ropes, slides and water cannons — parents climb aboard too. The hardest one to pull them away from.

  • Kiddie Slides

    Three shrunk-down slides in Kiddie Cove, one of which a parent can ride alongside a very small guest.

  • Octopus Pool

    Swing off the tentacles of a giant octopus, then cool down in the fountains. Swings hold 50 lb, so it's for the little ones only.

  • Yellow Submarine

    A wading pool built around a bright yellow sub you slide down the back of, under a constant falling spray.

Bag check

What actually matters today

  • Cash or card in a waterproof pouch
  • Sunscreen, and reapply after the wave pool
  • Water shoes for hot pavement
  • A towel each — nobody sharing
  • Locker money, decided at the gate
  • One phone designated for photos
  • Refillable bottle
  • Meeting spot: Surf City chairs

If the queues turn brutal, the H2Go pass covers nine of these rides — Bootlegger's Run, Alien Invasion, Dr. Von Dark's, Dragon's Den, Dinosaur Falls, Giant Twister, Hollywood Stunt Rider, Hyperlight and the Lazy River — and Riptide Racer takes the Speedy Pass. Worth pricing at the gate before we commit to the schedule.
