10:00

Gates open: burn the big three first

These three eat the longest lines by noon and all three are 48″-and-up, so do them while the park is still filling in. Walk right, not left — most people drift toward the wave pool first.

  1. 01

    Bootlegger's Run

    Water coaster

    New York's only hydromagnetic water coaster: a four-person inline raft that gets blasted uphill between drops, the biggest of them from nearly five stories. It's the single best ride in the park and the line only gets worse — this is stop one, no debate.

    • Height48″
    • Crew2–4 per raft
    • Nerve
  2. 02

    Bombs Away

    Drop capsule

    You stand in a capsule 50 feet up, the floor opens, and you're gone — roughly 26 feet per second down 300 feet of slide, racing whoever's dumb enough to stand in the capsule next to you. Best photo op of the day, if someone stays at the bottom.

    • Height48″
    • FormatSide-by-side racer
    • Nerve
  3. 03

    Riptide Racer

    Mat racer

    Four lanes, head-first, face down on a mat. The only ride here where the whole group settles something at once, so pick your bragging rights before you climb the stairs.

    • Height48″
    • Lanes4 abreast
    • Nerve

11:30

The dark block

Three enclosed rides in a row. If anyone in the group hates the pitch-black tubes, this is the stretch to sit out — send them to the Lazy River and regroup at noon.

  1. 04

    Dr. Von Dark's Tunnel of Terror

    Dark funnel

    Pitch-dark tube into a 40-foot drop, then a wall you scale sideways before gravity takes it back. You cannot see any of it coming, which is the entire point.

    • HeightPosted at ride
    • LightsNone
    • Nerve
  2. 05

    Alien Invasion

    Tornado funnel

    Dark tube feeding a giant funnel — you oscillate up one wall, hang for a beat, and drop out the bottom. Heavier rafts swing higher, so load up.

    • Height48″
    • RideShared tube
    • Nerve
  3. 06

    Hyperlight

    Light & sound

    The old Abyss, rebuilt as a fully enclosed tube with synced light and music — the water moves with the sound. Ride it single if you want the effects, double if you want to yell about them.

If anyone brings kids

The shallow end of the park

Four kid-scaled areas, all clustered away from the thrill towers. Any of them works as a base camp if the group needs to split for an hour.

  • Stingray Bay

    The newest addition — a water playground with more than fifteen ways to get soaked, between the sprayers and the pint-sized slides.

  • Kiddie Cove

    Full-size slides shrunk down to kid scale, so they get the same ride the rest of us are queueing an hour for.

  • Octopus Pool

    Inside Kiddie Cove: a giant octopus with tentacles to swing off. Shade and seating right there for whoever's on duty.

  • Monsoon Lagoon

    Shipwreck pool with a slide and a pile of pirate-themed water cannons. Loud, chaotic, and the hardest one to pull them away from.
